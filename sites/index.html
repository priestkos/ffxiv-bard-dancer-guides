Choose a Path

Three jobs, three roles, three instincts. Bard rewards rigid timer discipline and a single perfectly-timed snapshot. Dancer rewards resource hoarding and a twenty-second detonation. Scholar rewards resource discipline and knowing when to spend a stack on damage or a heal. All three are strongest when the person behind them has stopped thinking about the buttons.

02

The Shared Inheritance

Bard and Dancer share a foundation from being Physical Ranged: no cast times, total freedom of movement, and a two-minute raid buff that other jobs plan their own bursts around. Scholar shares the two-minute spine as a healer, with Chain Stratagem as its raid contribution.

Movement

Nothing stops you casting

Bard and Dancer have no cast times. Scholar's Biolysis and Art of War are instant too, so across all three jobs you can reposition through mechanics without losing a single GCD. The only excuse for a dropped global is your own attention.

Cadence

A 120-second spine

All three live on the two-minute cycle: Bard's Raging Strikes and Radiant Finale, Dancer's Technical Finish and Devilment, Scholar's Chain Stratagem. The rest of each job exists to feed that window.

Contribution

Paid in other people's damage

Each carries a low personal ceiling and a raid buff. Your parse lives and dies on how well the party converts the window you hand them.
